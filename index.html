What I owned, and how it was built

One Cash

2026 · present

Yemen's digital wallet. I build both Android apps — the customer wallet and the agent app — and wrote the AI chat assistant that ships inside the customer app.

Chat SDK · sole author

  • An AI assistant packaged as a reusable Compose library (AAR via JitPack), embedded in the customer app's home screen.
  • Replies stream over an OkHttp WebSocket that reconnects with exponential backoff.
  • Messages queue in Room while the socket is down and replay when it reopens.
  • Session state in EncryptedSharedPreferences, behind a single-entry facade.

Customer app

  • Loyalty points end to end: new home experience, hero module and points redemption.
  • Multi-biller payment journeys with dynamic biller forms, checkout and confirmation.
  • Notification events and in-app notification handling.
  • Side-menu reporting, favourites, and Arabic/English localisation throughout.

Agent app

  • Cash-in, e-wallet and over-the-counter transfers, refunds and nominated-bank transfers.
  • Vouchers, e-cards, Zakat payments and service orders.
  • Registration and MSISDN validation extended to customers outside Yemen.
  • Transaction history and detail rebuilt, including refund journeys and request hashing.

myFirst Cam — AI video generation

2024–2026 · feature owner

Children pick photos, record a voice note, and get a finished video back. I owned the pipeline end to end, on a kids' device where failures have to clean up after themselves.

  • Parallel uploads to Firebase Storage under a concurrency limit, with full cleanup on failure.
  • Generation brokered through Firestore, surfaced as a step-based progress UI.
  • Voiceover, music and original audio mixed into the render with FFmpeg.
  • Template renders scheduled on WorkManager so long jobs survive backgrounding.
