Selected work

What I owned, and how it was built.

OneCash AI chat SDK

2026 — sole author

An AI assistant packaged as a drop-in Compose library, so every app in the group gets the same chat experience without rebuilding it. Published as an AAR through JitPack with its own theming, navigation and service flows.

  • Replies stream over an OkHttp WebSocket that reconnects with exponential backoff.
  • Messages queue in Room while the socket is down and replay the moment it reopens.
  • Session state is held in EncryptedSharedPreferences behind a single-entry facade.
  • Bilingual Arabic and English, with full right-to-left layout.

myFirst Cam — AI video generation

2024–2026 — feature owner

Children pick photos, record a voice note, and get a finished video back. I owned the pipeline end to end, on a kids' device where failures have to clean up after themselves.

  • Parallel uploads to Firebase Storage under a concurrency limit, with full artefact cleanup when a run fails.
  • Generation requests brokered through Firestore, surfaced as a step-based progress UI.
  • Voiceover, music and original audio mixed into the render with FFmpeg.
  • Template renders scheduled on WorkManager so long jobs survive backgrounding.
  • Daily generation quotas driven by Remote Config, so AI cost is tuned without a release.
